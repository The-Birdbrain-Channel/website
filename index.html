Who are we?

We are an indie channel that streams a range of content:

On The Birdbrain Channel - Channel 1, we stream weather covering the Orlando, Florida area. Usually an i2Jr, but sometimes an i1 emulator or freestar!


On The Birdbrain Channel - Channel 2, we usually stream a DASDEC (nation-wide alerts) or a ZAP2IT guide (data from mist live!), occasionally streaming national weather!
